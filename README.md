# Ears

An iOS app for people with cochlear implants and hearing aids.

Conversations move fast, and missing a few words can mean missing the point. Ears puts live captions on your phone and tells you who said what. It also has short listening practice, so the time between appointments counts for something.

## What it does

- Live captions for the conversation in front of you.
- Speaker names. Enroll the people you talk to most, and their names show up on the captions.
- Name alerts. Your phone flags it when someone says your name.
- Listening practice, including accents.

## What it doesn't do

It never diagnoses. Ears won't tell you something is wrong with your hearing or your device. It gives you ways to practice.

## Status

Launching in the App Store in November 2026. Built with feedback from beta testers in the cochlear implant community.
